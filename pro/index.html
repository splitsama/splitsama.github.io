Get SplitSama Pro

Splitting stays free and unlimited. Pro adds the extras. Compare Free and Pro.

Monthly subscription, or a trip pass. Pro is a monthly subscription you can cancel anytime in Google Play. A trip pass is a one-off payment for one group. The app shows prices in your local currency before you buy, under Account, then Pro.

Pro

Smarter entry

Big bills take the longest to enter, so let the app do the typing. Say the expense out loud, or scan a receipt to fill in the amounts, then give each dish to the person who had it.

  • Voice add: say an expense, and it asks what was missed and fills it in
  • Scan receipts and split item by item
  • Open bills: everyone picks the items they had (try free in 2 groups)
  • Default splits for each group
Pro

Who pays next

Stop arguing over whose turn it is. Each group suggests who should pay next, based on what everyone has paid so far, so turns stay fair without anyone keeping score.

Pro

Insights and control

See where your money goes, by category, month and group, and find any past expense in seconds. Group owners also get tools to keep things tidy.

  • Charts, insights and search
  • Group export to Excel and PDF
  • Owner-only editing for groups
Pro

Trip mode

See daily totals while you travel, who spent what, and a day-by-day timeline, then share a recap with the group when you get home.

Pro

Shared budgets

Set a budget for a group or a category and get alerts at 80% and 100%, so spending never comes as a surprise.

Pro

Live currency conversion

Abroad or in a mixed-currency group? See totals in your home currency at live rates, and pay foreign debts in your own currency.

Pro for the whole group

Group features work for everyone in the group, even friends without Pro.

One-off

Trip pass

Going on one trip? Buy a trip pass for that group and everyone in it gets trip mode, budgets, who pays next, open bills, split by item, default splits and group export for 30 days. It's a single payment that doesn't renew.

Pro

Share Pro with your groups

With Pro, turn on sharing for up to 3 groups, and everyone in them gets the same group features while you have Pro.

Free and Pro compared

Everything you need to split bills is free. Pro adds tools that save time and show where your money goes.
