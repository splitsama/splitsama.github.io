Privacy Policy

How SplitSama collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

About this policy

SplitSama has no ads, and your expenses are end-to-end encrypted: only the people you share them with can read them, not us. This policy explains what the SplitSama app for Android (app ID com.cadence.splitsama) collects, why, and the choices you have.

SplitSama is made by SplitSama, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Questions about privacy: splitsama@gmail.com.

By using SplitSama you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Service.

Your expenses are stored on your phone. When you share a group or expense, it is encrypted on your phone before it leaves.

How sync works

  • End-to-end encrypted. Changes are encrypted on your phone before they are sent. Our relay, which runs on Cloudflare, stores only encrypted data and the usage counts described below. It cannot see names, amounts, groups or who wrote what. The one exception is email invites: see "Email invites" below.
  • What the relay processes. As expected for a relay, it processes your device's IP address and device push token. The IP address is used to deliver data and for short, per-minute limits that stop floods of requests; the relay does not keep request logs or store IP addresses with your data. The push token is kept with the encrypted mailboxes you belong to so the relay can ping your phone, and is removed when you turn notifications off or the token stops working.
  • Usage counts. To protect the relay from abuse, it counts how much each sign-in pass writes in a day. A pass isn't linked to your account. The relay also counts how many passes each account requests in a day, and reduces that to totals when the day ends. Counts are deleted after 30 days.
  • Invite links and QR codes. In an open group, anyone with the link or QR code can join. With "Approve new members" on, people can only ask to join until a member approves them. Invite links open a page on our website, splitsama.app. The invite itself, which includes your name and the group's name, is in the part of the link that browsers do not send to any server, so the website never receives it.
  • Personal inboxes. Messages between people, such as non-group expenses, reminders and invitations, go to an encrypted inbox only the recipient can read. Inbox messages are deleted after 90 days.
  • Email invites. If you add a friend by email, your phone leaves an invite for them on the relay, so they can find you if they use SplitSama. The invite is encrypted, and contains what an invite link contains, including your name. The relay files it under a scrambled code made from the address, not the address itself, and deletes it after 14 days, or sooner if it's declined. Your friend's address is never used to contact them: we send no email.
  • Checking for email invites. While signed in, your phone checks for invites about once a day by sending your Google sign-in to the relay. The relay uses your address to find invites left for you and does not store it. It does not tell anyone whether an address uses SplitSama. Nothing is linked until you tap Accept. If you tap Decline, the invite is removed from the relay and the sender isn't told. The sender can send another invite after 14 days.
  • How email invites are protected. Email invites are designed to stay protected if the relay's stored data leaks, but not from the relay itself, because it sees your address when you check for invites.
  • Notifications. When something changes, the relay sends your phone a ping with no content, through Expo and Google's push services. Your phone writes the notification text itself.
  • Exchange rates and settings. The app downloads daily currency rates through our relay and app settings from Google Firebase. These requests send nothing about you or your expenses.
  • Network data. Like any internet service, Cloudflare, Google, Expo, RevenueCat and Sentry see your device's IP address when it connects. They handle it under their own privacy policies.

Backups

  • Google Drive. When you sign in with Google, SplitSama backs up an encrypted copy of your data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive every day. It only asks for access to that folder, not to your other Drive files.
  • How it is protected. The backup key is created by our relay from your verified Google sign-in, and nothing about it is stored per user. Google holds the encrypted backup but not the key, and our relay can make the key but never receives the backup, so neither can read it alone.
  • Backup file. You can also save a backup file protected by a passphrase you choose. Nobody, including us, can recover a lost passphrase.

Permissions and features on your phone

  • Camera. Used to scan invite QR codes and receipts.
  • Microphone. Used only while you use Voice add (a Pro feature), to hear the expense you describe. Your phone's own speech recognition turns your words into text. When the language is downloaded on your phone, this happens on the phone; otherwise your phone's speech service (for example Google's) may process the audio under its own privacy policy. SplitSama reads the text on your phone and does not record, keep or upload the audio or the words.
  • Photos. Used only when you pick a receipt image from your library.
  • Receipts. A receipt is scanned and read on your phone (using Google's on-device document scanner and ML Kit); its amounts go into the expense and the photo is deleted. Receipt photos are never uploaded or stored.
  • Contacts. Used only when you tap "Choose from contacts", to read the one contact you pick. We never upload your contact list.
  • Messages you paste. When you paste a bank, card or UPI message to make an expense, it is read on your phone and not uploaded.
  • Notifications. Used for group changes, comments, reminders and budget alerts. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Import and export. Splitwise files you import are read on your phone. CSV, Excel and PDF exports are made on your phone and go only where you share them.

Payments

Settle-up buttons open your payment app (for example UPI or PayPal) with the details filled in. Payments happen in that app; SplitSama never sees or stores card or bank details. SplitSama Pro is paid through Google Play, which handles your payment details.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete your account. In Account, tap Delete my account. You leave every group (others see "Deleted user", and their balances stay correct), groups you own lose their owner so every remaining member can edit them, your Google Drive backup is deleted, SplitSama's access to your Google account is revoked, and the data and keys on this phone are erased. If you use other phones, sign them out first from Your devices. This cannot be undone.
  • Log out or sign out a phone. Logging out erases SplitSama's data on that phone, and your backup stays in Drive. From Devices, you can sign out another of your phones; it erases its data the next time it connects.
  • On friends' phones. Expenses you shared stay on the phones of the people you shared them with, as in any shared ledger. You can delete expenses and comments you are part of. Deleting a group removes it for everyone in it.
  • On the relay. Encrypted group data stays on the relay so members, including new ones, can sync. If nobody uses a group for 12 months, its data is removed from the relay; members' phones still have it and send it again if the group is used later. Inbox messages expire after 90 days. We cannot read relay data or tell whose it is.
  • Email invites. Invites you leave are deleted from the relay after 14 days or when declined, including if you delete your account sooner.
  • Subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel SplitSama Pro. Cancel it in Google Play; billing records are kept by Google as the law requires.

Step-by-step instructions are on our account deletion page. If you can no longer open the app, email splitsama@gmail.com and we will help. You can also remove SplitSama's backup from Google Drive in Drive settings under Manage apps, and remove its access in your Google account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete your information, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to use these rights.

Security, children and changes

Security. Shared data and backups are protected with strong encryption, and every change is signed by its author's device key. No method is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked and your invite links private.

Children. SplitSama is for people aged 18 or over. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Where information is processed. Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards.

Changes. We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact. SplitSama, splitsama@gmail.com.
