How sync works

  • End-to-end encrypted. Changes are encrypted on your phone before they are sent. Our relay, which runs on Cloudflare, stores only encrypted data. It cannot see names, amounts, groups or who wrote what.
  • What the relay processes. As expected for a relay, it processes your device's IP address and device push token. The IP address is used to deliver data and for short, per-minute limits that stop floods of requests; the relay does not keep request logs or store IP addresses with your data. The push token is kept with the encrypted mailboxes you belong to so the relay can ping your phone, and is removed when you turn notifications off or the token stops working.
  • Invite links and QR codes. In an open group, anyone with the link or QR code can join. With "Approve new members" on, people can only ask to join until a member approves them.
  • Personal inboxes. Messages between people, such as non-group expenses, reminders and invitations, go to an encrypted inbox only the recipient can read. Inbox messages are deleted after 90 days.
  • Notifications. When something changes, the relay sends your phone a ping with no content, through Expo and Apple or Google's push services. Your phone writes the notification text itself.
  • Exchange rates and settings. The app downloads daily currency rates through our relay and app settings from Google Firebase. These requests send nothing about you or your expenses.
  • Network data. Like any internet service, Cloudflare, Google, Expo, RevenueCat and Sentry see your device's IP address when it connects. They handle it under their own privacy policies.

Backups

  • Google Drive. When you sign in with Google, SplitSama backs up an encrypted copy of your data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive every day. It only asks for access to that folder, not to your other Drive files.
  • How it is protected. The backup key is created by our relay from your verified Google sign-in, and nothing about it is stored per user. Google holds the encrypted backup but not the key, and our relay can make the key but never receives the backup, so neither can read it alone.
  • Backup file. You can also save a backup file protected by a passphrase you choose. Nobody, including us, can recover a lost passphrase.

Permissions and features on your phone

  • Camera. Used to scan invite QR codes and receipts. The microphone is not used.
  • Photos. Used only when you pick a receipt image from your library.
  • Receipts. A receipt is scanned and read on your phone (Apple Vision on iOS; Google's on-device document scanner and ML Kit on Android); its amounts go into the expense and the photo is deleted. Receipt photos are never uploaded or stored.
  • Contacts. Used only when you tap "Choose from contacts", to read the one contact you pick. We never upload your contact list.
  • Messages you paste. When you paste a bank, card or UPI message to make an expense, it is read on your phone and not uploaded.
  • Notifications. Used for group changes, comments, reminders and budget alerts. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Import and export. Splitwise files you import are read on your phone. CSV, Excel and PDF exports are made on your phone and go only where you share them.

Payments

Settle-up buttons open your payment app (for example UPI or PayPal) with the details filled in. Payments happen in that app; SplitSama never sees or stores card or bank details. SplitSama Pro is paid through Google Play or the App Store, which handle your payment details.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete your account. In Account, tap Delete my account. You leave every group (others see "Deleted user", and their balances stay correct), your Google Drive backup is deleted, SplitSama's access to your Google account is revoked, and the data and keys on this phone are erased. If you use other phones, sign them out first from Your devices. This cannot be undone.
  • Log out or sign out a phone. Logging out erases SplitSama's data on that phone, and your backup stays in Drive. From Devices, you can sign out another of your phones; it erases its data the next time it connects.
  • On friends' phones. Expenses you shared stay on the phones of the people you shared them with, as in any shared ledger. You can delete expenses and comments you are part of. Deleting a group removes it for everyone in it.
  • On the relay. Encrypted group data stays on the relay so members, including new ones, can sync. Inbox messages expire after 90 days. We cannot read relay data or tell whose it is.
  • Subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel SplitSama Pro. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store; billing records are kept by them as the law requires.

Step-by-step instructions are on our account deletion page. If you can no longer open the app, email splitsama@gmail.com and we will help. You can also remove SplitSama's backup from Google Drive in Drive settings under Manage apps, and remove its access in your Google account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete your information, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to use these rights.

Security, children and changes

Security. Shared data and backups are encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305, and every change is signed by its author's device key. No method is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked and your invite links private.

Children. SplitSama is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Where information is processed. Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards.

Changes. We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
