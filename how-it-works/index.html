How do I use SplitSama?

Split bills with friends, flatmates and trips in a few steps.

1. Sign in

Install SplitSama on Android and sign in with Google. An encrypted backup then goes to your own Google Drive every day.

2. Make a group or add a friend

Make a group for your flat or trip, or add a friend for costs between the two of you. Invite people by link or QR code. With "Approve new members" on, people can only ask to join until a member approves them.

3. Add expenses as they happen

Enter what it was for, how much, and who paid. Split it equally, by exact amounts, percentages or shares, or adjust one person up or down. Several people can pay for one expense, and each expense keeps its own currency.

4. See who owes whom

SplitSama keeps every balance up to date, and works out the fewest payments needed to settle up a whole group. You can add expenses offline, and they sync when you are back online.

5. Settle up

Open UPI, PayPal, Venmo or Cash App with the amount filled in, open Revolut to the right person, or record cash. SplitSama does not move money: it opens your payment app for you.
