Everything you need to split expenses

Everything on this page is free and unlimited, with no ads. Pro adds extras on top.

Split any way

Real bills are rarely even, so split them equally, by exact amounts, percentages, shares or adjustments. If two people paid for dinner, add them both as payers. There's no daily limit, so you can add every expense as it happens.

Simplify debts

In a busy group, everyone can end up owing everyone. SplitSama works out the fewest payments needed to settle the whole group, so a tangle of small debts becomes just a few simple payments.

Settle up in a tap

When it's time to pay, SplitSama opens UPI, PayPal, Venmo or Cash App with the amount filled in, or opens Revolut to the right person. Paid in cash? Just record it. No typing amounts, and no awkward "how much was it again?" messages.

Groups for flats and trips

Invite people by link or QR code, and approve new members if you want control over who joins. Comment on an expense to clear up questions in the app. Reminders and notifications keep everyone up to date without you chasing them.

Personal expenses

Track rent, electricity and other bills that are yours alone, by month and category. They stay private and are never shared with a group. You get your own spending and your shared costs in one app.

Everything in one place

Activity shows your latest expenses across all your groups and personal bills. Filter it to find what you need, and see at a glance what you lent or borrowed. It's the quick way to check what changed since you last looked.

And the rest, also free

Dashboard

Your overall balance, this month's spending and who owes whom, at a glance.

58 currencies, offline

Each expense keeps its own currency, which is handy abroad. Add costs with no signal, and they sync later.

Edit history

See who changed what on every expense, so nothing changes quietly.

Export anytime

Export all your expenses to Excel, PDF or CSV.

End-to-end encrypted

Only the people in a group can read its expenses. Receipts are read on your phone and the photo is deleted.

Backed up to your Drive

An encrypted backup goes to your own Google Drive every day.
