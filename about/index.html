About SplitSama

Split bills. Stay friends.

What SplitSama is

SplitSama is an app for sharing costs with friends, flatmates and on trips. It keeps track of who owes whom, so nobody has to do the maths or feel awkward asking.

What we care about

  • Splitting stays free and unlimited, with no ads
  • Shared expenses are end-to-end encrypted, so only the people in a group can read them
  • Your data backs up to your own Google Drive, encrypted

Who makes it

SplitSama is made by an independent developer.
