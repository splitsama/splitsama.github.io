A free bill splitting app, with no limits

Add as many expenses and groups as you like, every day.

Trips, flats, every group

Keep each trip, flat and friend group in its own place, so costs never get mixed up. Invite people by link or QR code, and they can join in seconds. You can see what you owe or are owed in every group at a glance, without digging through chats.

Split any way

Real bills are rarely even, so split them equally, by exact amounts, percentages, shares or adjustments. If two people paid for dinner, add them both as payers. Everyone pays their fair share, and nobody has to do the maths.

Settle up in a tap

When it's time to pay, SplitSama opens UPI, PayPal, Venmo or Cash App with the amount filled in, or opens Revolut to the right person. Paid in cash? Just record it. No typing amounts, and no awkward "how much was it again?" messages.

Personal expenses

Track rent, electricity and other bills that are yours alone, by month and category. They stay private and are never shared with a group. You get your own spending and your shared costs in one app.

Questions about splitting bills

What is an expense splitting app?

An expense splitting app records shared costs and works out who owes whom. SplitSama is a free expense splitting app for friends, flatmates and trips: you add bills as they happen, and it keeps every balance up to date.

How do I split a bill with friends?

Add the expense in SplitSama, choose who paid and who shares it, and split it equally, by exact amounts, percentages or shares. SplitSama keeps a running balance, so everyone can see who owes whom.

Is SplitSama free?

Yes. Splitting is free and unlimited, with no ads. An optional Pro plan adds extras such as receipt scanning, trip mode and budgets.

Can I split bills with flatmates and on trips?

Yes. Make a group for your flat or trip, add expenses as they happen, and SplitSama works out the fewest payments needed to settle up.

Does it work with UPI?

Yes. Settle up opens UPI with the amount filled in. It also works with PayPal, Venmo, Cash App and Revolut, or you can record a cash payment.

Can I move from Splitwise?

Yes. With Pro, you can import a group from a Splitwise export.

Are my expenses private?

Yes. Shared expenses are end-to-end encrypted, so only the people in a group can read them.

Does SplitSama work offline?

Yes. You can add expenses without a connection, and they sync when you're back online.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. SplitSama is for Android for now.
